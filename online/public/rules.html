Official rules

TYPEO is a live venue word game. Guess five-letter words, earn balls, then try to win bingo in the finale. These rules match the online app used at the show.

Joining the game

  • Open the play link shown on the venue screen or scan the QR code. If the host gave an event code, use that same link — it may look like /play?event=yourcode.
  • Guest: pick a display name and tap Join game. No account required.
  • Account: create one or log in if you want your name saved between shows.
  • Join the lobby before the host starts a round so your name appears on the big screen.
  • Use one phone per player. Your guesses and bingo card stay tied to that device.
When a round goes live, the app tries to bring you straight into the game if you already signed in with a name.

TYPEO rounds

Each round is one secret five-letter word chosen by the host.

Guessing phase

  • The room sees the first letter of the word (for example, S ● ● ● ●).
  • Type a valid five-letter English word and submit before the timer runs out.
  • You may change your guess during the same guessing window — your latest submission is the one that counts.
  • Words must be in the game dictionary. Invalid guesses do not count as a solve and show Not a word….
  • If you submit the correct word during guessing, you are locked in for that word — no further guesses that round.

Results phase

  • When time is up (or everyone has submitted), the host reveals results.
  • You see color and symbol feedback for your official guess (see below).
  • Players who guessed the word correctly earn balls from the pot.

Round end

  • The round keeps going through multiple guess windows while balls remain in the pot and at least one player has not solved yet.
  • The round ends when the word is fully solved out or the final two-ball guess window finishes. The answer is then revealed to the room.

Guess feedback

Every letter in your guess is marked one of three ways:

▪ Correct spot — right letter in the right position.
○ Wrong spot — letter is in the word, but not in that position.
✕ Not in word — letter is not in the answer.

Duplicate letters follow standard Lingo rules: a letter is only marked present or correct as many times as it appears in the answer.

On your phone, the on-screen keyboard remembers greens and yellows from valid guesses in the current round.

Balls & scoring

Balls are the currency of the show. You collect them during TYPEO and spend them during bingo.

The pot

  • Every guess window is worth 5 balls until at least one player solves the word. The opening window adds a +1 bonus (6 balls total). Double-ball mode doubles all of these values.
  • If nobody solves on the opening guess, only the bonus drops — the next window is still 5 balls, and it stays at 5 for as many windows as it takes until someone solves.
  • Once someone solves, they earn the stake on the table (6 on the opening guess, otherwise 5). The pot then drops by one ball per solve until the final 2-ball window.
  • The last guess window is always at 2 balls. After that window, the word ends.

Earning balls

  • Solve the word on a reveal and you earn the full stake that was on the table for that reveal.
  • The first correct solver in a results phase gets a shout-out on the venue screen.
  • The host may award +1 ball to everyone as a bonus (sometimes called Communism / Ballshevik).

Typical stake ladder

With normal (1×) balls, a round often looks like this:

Guess window Pot stake
Opening guess6 balls (5 + opening bonus)
While nobody has solved yet5 balls (may repeat)
After first solve4 balls
After later solves3 → 2 balls
Final window2 balls, then word ends
If double-ball mode is on, multiply every stake above by two (opening pot 12, final window 4, and so on).

Multiple guesses in one round

  • A round is one word, but it can have several timed guess windows back-to-back.
  • After each results phase, players who have not solved yet get another chance while balls remain.
  • Each new window uses the updated pot stake. Your phone keeps a history of every guess you made that round.
  • Your official result on the venue screen is the guess locked in when results were revealed.
  • If you miss a window entirely, that counts as no guess for that stake.

Bingo finale

After TYPEO, the host starts bingo. Your phone switches to your bingo card automatically.

Your card

  • Every player gets a unique 5×5 card based on the game and your name.
  • The center square is a free space.
  • Called numbers light up on their own — you do not tap squares to mark them.

Winning

  • You need at least 1 ball earned from TYPEO to be eligible.
  • Your ball count is your bingo budget: you must complete a line by the time that many numbers have been called.
  • Example: if you earned 4 balls, you need bingo by call 4 (or earlier).
  • A valid line is any row, column, or diagonal — same as classic bingo.
  • When you have a qualifying line, tap BINGO! on your phone. First valid claim wins.
  • If your line completes after your budget runs out, you are out of the running — keep playing for fun, but you cannot win.

Finale scramble

During bingo you may see an eight-letter scramble on your phone — a fun side puzzle tied to the show. Unscrambling it is optional and does not replace calling bingo.

At the venue

  • The big screen shows the word clue, timer, player cards, and who still needs to submit.
  • Your name and status appear on the player board once you join.
  • The host can broadcast messages to the room and to phones — watch for those cues.
  • A leaderboard may appear between rounds when the host turns it on.
  • Keep your phone brightness up and stay on the play page during the show.

Host-controlled features

These are decided live by the host — not every show uses all of them:

  • Timer length for guessing and between rounds
  • Pause / resume timer
  • Double-ball mode (2× stakes)
  • Bonus ball for all players
  • Champion / monarch crown for a returning winner
  • Starting bingo and calling numbers
Fair play: one person, one device, one name. Do not share answers across the room before the reveal.
